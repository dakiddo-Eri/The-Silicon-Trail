# The-Silicon-Trail

This game is based on The Oregon Trail game, but in the future and AI.
hehehe

In the year 2029, most datacenters now power AI, taking up all the space for
other important datacenters and other buildings for people. You are assigned
a job to carry a stick of DDR6 32 GB of RAM to a health datacenter that has
been recently shutdown because of an AI hallucination. Well... Onwards you go!!
